Into ☥ Egypt

How to Play

  • Name your pharaoh and choose a difficulty to begin.
  • Each turn brings an event that may help or hinder you. Some events ask you to choose.
  • On your turn, you can fight a neighboring army, recruit soldiers, build a wonder, or consult the oracle. You can fight and consult the oracle only once per turn.
  • Every 1,000 slaves earn you 1 gold per turn. Once built, wonders pay out every turn.
  • You lose if your gold drops below zero, your army is wiped out, or your authority runs out.
  • Survive 20 turns on Easy, 35 on Medium, or 50 on Hard to finish your reign.
  • Finish with enough glory, and history will remember you. Otherwise, you'll be forgotten.
  • Save your game between turns. To continue later, type your pharaoh's name on the title screen and load it.
